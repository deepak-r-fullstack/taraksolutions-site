AgentGuard

Live demo

Lets AI agents work on real company systems, with a person in charge. Every action is checked against the company's rules, risky ones wait for a manager, everything is recorded, and mistakes can be undone with one click.

  • Rules are checked by plain code, so an AI can never talk its way into an approval.
  • Safe against double clicks and retries: twenty Undo clicks at once undo exactly once.
  • Every step recorded: which agent, for whom, which rule, who approved.

React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, MongoDB. 50 automated tests.
